Can 0–5 bars come from papers?

Yes, but only when a paper reports a named scale or a quantity we can rescale with a written formula. HPLC concentrations, EUC, and “tastes umami” sentences stay in the published-measurements table. They are not turned into earthy or fibrous bars.

Rule for a bar

  1. Open the paper. Confirm species, sample, and the scale.
  2. Copy the number as published (keep the original unit in the measurements table).
  3. If the scale is not 0–5, rescale with a formula shown on the page. Example: Phat 2016 uses 1–11, so (score − 1) × 5 / 10.
  4. One axis per measurement. A paper that only scored umami does not fill texture.
  5. Do not average papers or batches unless the paper does.

Source. Phat, Moon & Lee (2016) Table 3. Ten trained panellists scored a 1% boiling-water extract of freeze-dried Korean market oyster mushroom on an 11-point umami scale (1 = very weak, 6 = medium, 11 = very strong). Mean 9.33 ± 1.51.

Rescale. (9.33 − 1) × 5 / 10 = 4.2 on 0–5.

What I will not do

  • Fill earthy / fibrous / meat-like from “sounds like chicken.”
  • Turn EUC (mg MSG/g) into a 0–5 bar without a stated mapping you approve.
  • Mix Phat’s 11-point human scores with the electronic-tongue units on the same axis.
